01 / The philosophy

My Aesthetic
Philosophy

A streetlight in the fog.
Something ancient beneath the water.

My aesthetic lives where noir meets Lovecraftian horror: human doubt set against an unknowable universe. It is intimate and immense, elegant and unsettling.

Explore the two worlds ↓

The meeting point

The city has secrets.
The universe owes us nothing.

Noir gives this world its human scale: rain on pavement, a half-lit face, a choice with no clean answer. Lovecraftian horror opens a fissure beneath it. The mystery is no longer just who did it, but what kind of world could allow it.

I am drawn to that tension. The silhouette of a detective and the suggestion of Cthulhu belong in the same frame: one searching for the truth, the other making truth feel impossibly small.
